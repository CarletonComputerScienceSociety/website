Aryan's Platform

Hi everyone! My name is Aryan, but I also go by Neo, and I’m running to be your First-Year Representative for the Carleton Computer Science Society.

Since starting at Carleton, I’ve loved how quickly this community has come together. The sheer number of people already reaching out to share notes, ask questions, or just talk through a hard concept has honestly been one of my favorite parts of first year so far. I want to run because I think that spirit of “we’re figuring this out together” is something worth building on, and I’d love the chance to help make it even stronger for everyone.

If elected, here’s what I’d want to focus on:

  • Peer learning support. A lot of us are strongest in different areas, some people pick up math fast, others are more comfortable with programming or security concepts right away. I’d like to help create more organized ways for students to connect with each other for study groups, tutoring, or just quick help when something isn’t clicking, so nobody feels like they’re stuck figuring it out completely alone.
  • A shared resource space. Something simple like a place where notes, past assignment breakdowns, and helpful study material can live so incoming and current students have a starting point instead of starting from scratch every time.
  • A real link between students and the society. I want to make sure first-year voices such as questions about courses, concerns about workload, ideas for events actually reach the people who can act on them, not just float around in group chats. I’d check in regularly with our year to make sure what I’m advocating for reflects what people actually need.
  • More ways to build community around what we love. I’d love to help organize more low-pressure events to bring out the passion of computers and hacking in my friends and classmates. Things like casual CTFs, project nights, or study socials sound good so we get more chances to bond over the stuff that brought us here in the first place, not just in classes but outside of them too.

None of this is about doing something completely new or extraordinary, it’s about supporting and growing what’s already working here, and making sure first-years especially feel like they have someone looking out for them as they settle in.

I care about this because I’ve already found myself naturally helping people since I got here, and I’d love the opportunity to do that in a way that reaches further and helps build something lasting for our whole year. I’d be honored to represent you all this year, and I promise to always be approachable, honest, and easy to reach if you ever have something on your mind.

Thank you for reading, and I hope I can earn your vote!

Neo.
