Amy Xie's Platform

Hey everyone! 👋 My name is Amy, I’m from Ottawa, and I’m a first-year computer science student super excited to meet and become friends with other students here at Carleton. That’s why I’d love to be YOUR first-year rep!

First year is full of new classes, new people, and new opportunities. You’ll probably meet a lot of people, but actually feeling like you are part of the CS community can be harder.

As First-Year Representative, I want to make sure first-years are heard, help people find their place in the community, and bring your ideas and concerns to CCSS. Here’s how I’ll do that for us:

1. Listen 👂

I don’t want to sit in meetings and just assume I know what every first-year wants. I want to ask you. I’ll be easy to reach through Discord, email, and other social media, and I want to make sure your ideas and concerns actually get heard!

  • Got an event idea? Let me know!
  • Think something could be improved? Tell me!
  • Is there something first-years are concerned about? I want to make sure it reaches the CCSS Board

As first-year rep, let’s start by connecting and actually talking about how we want our first year of computer science to look 😄

2. Community 🤝

You should not need to arrive at Carleton with ten side projects, an internship, and five programming languages under your belt to feel like you belong in the CS community.

I want to help create more ways for first-years to meet people, learn together, and get involved without feeling like they already need to know everyone or know everything.

That can look like:

  • Game nights and other low-pressure social events
  • Study sessions where first-years can work together and get to know each other
  • Beginner-friendly workshops and learning opportunities
  • Other new ideas that come directly from first-year students (you!)

3. Act 💥

Listening only matters if we make it happen afterwards. I will bring first-year ideas and concerns directly to the CCSS Board and work with the team to turn ideas into actual events, initiatives, or changes.

I also want to follow up with first-years so you know what happened with the ideas you brought forward - not just collect suggestions and let them disappear.
