Hey there, my name is Amy (short for Amethyst), and I’m a first year BCS student (like you! – unless you’re in B.Cyber lol) under the Game Development stream while aiming for a minor in Music. I drove here all the way from Toronto with very little knowledge of the Ottawa area, and I’ve been trying my best to adjust to all the drastic changes that come with moving and going to university. By running as a First-Year Representative, I want to do my best to make starting out in such a different environment as fun and as simple as possible for everyone by:

* **Making the CCSS (and CU as a whole) even more welcoming:**<br/>
  Talking to new people, especially those much older than you, can be a very difficult and scary experience when being thrown into a brand new environment such as university (trust me, I would know). I’ll do my best to make everyone feel welcome and comfortable being themselves in not just the lounge and events, but all over campus! (I also regular the GSRC lounge btw, come visit sometime)

* **Making first-year voices heard:**<br/>
  Being a fellow first-year, I want to make the first-year experience with CCSS as great as possible by representing every suggestion or concern that first-years may have at internal meetings so that we may adjust accordingly! I would love to hear anything you have to say about the club or its events, so please reach out to me!

* **Organizing study groups and tutoring:**<br/>
  I absolutely adore working with computers and love talking about them even more! I want to assist as many students in computer science as I can by being available in the CCSS lounge to talk about programming whenever possible, and organizing scheduled study groups once exams draw near so we can all catch up together!

* **Assisting with and creating new CCSS events:**<br/>
  The CCSS hosts many fun events that are excellent for first-year students, and experiencing them myself inspired me to contribute and make them even better! For example, the Amazing Race was, in my opinion, the highlight of this year’s Week of Awesome, so I want to create more fun group events like it to make meeting people as a first-year feel effortless. I’ll also eventually host educational things like workshops or talks involving computer science!

* **Hosting game nights and working with the CCSS arcade machine:**<br/>
  As you could imagine from me being in the Game Development stream, I love both playing and creating games! I want to occasionally bring more chill social events to the table as a way to unwind from classes through game nights where we can play suggested games together, or even some of my own games! I also think the arcade machine is really cool and I may make some games specifically designed for it in the future :3
